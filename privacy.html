Privacy

How this website handles the information you share with us.

What we collect

If you use the contact form on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, your email address, your phone number if you choose to include it, and your message. Browsing the website does not require you to provide any personal information.

How it is used

Contact form submissions go to the Ahoskie Energy project team and are used to respond to your question or comment. That is the only use.

How it is stored

Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this website. Submissions are retained so the project team can follow up and keep a record of community questions.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not use advertising cookies, and this website does not run third party tracking or analytics scripts.

Questions

If you have a question about this page or want a message you submitted removed, email info@ahoskieenergy.com.
